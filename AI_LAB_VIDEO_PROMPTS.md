# AI Lab video prompts

These four prompts are ready to use with a video generation tool. If the tool accepts a reference image, upload the matching image from `public/ai-lab/` to keep the visual style consistent. The videos have not been generated yet.

## 1. Limestone tide — landscape, 16:9

Reference image: `limestone-tide.jpg`

> An eight-second cinematic shot of pale limestone terraces descending into a turquoise sea at dawn. The camera glides slowly sideways along the lowest terrace, revealing a distant crescent-shaped stone opening. Soft clouds drift through the opening, thin mist moves above the water, and small ripples catch the warm morning light. Keep the architecture stable and the motion calm and continuous. Photographic texture, sea-glass teal, chalk and warm sand. One unbroken shot, no people, no text, no logos, no sudden cuts, no morphing stone or flickering reflections.

## 2. Glass bloom — portrait, 9:16

Reference image: `glass-bloom.jpg`

> An eight-second macro film of a translucent glass flower growing from dark moss beside shallow water. The camera makes a very slow push-in while tiny droplets travel across the petals and a gentle breeze moves the surrounding moss. Warm highlights pass through the glass, creating delicate reflections in the water. Preserve the flower's shape and scale throughout. Moody olive and ivory fine-art photography. One continuous shot, no people, no text, no logos, no melting glass, no extra petals appearing, no abrupt camera movement.

## 3. Silver tide — portrait, 9:16

Reference image: `silver-tide.jpg`

> An eight-second blue-hour coastal film: a long ribbon of mirror-polished silver fabric floats above a black sand shore. A steady ocean breeze makes the ribbon twist slowly and naturally while gentle waves reach the sand below. The camera tracks forward at walking pace toward the distant rock spires; the moon remains fixed in the dusky sky. Keep the ribbon as one continuous object with realistic reflections. Cinematic teal, silver and muted peach. One unbroken shot, no people, no text, no logos, no duplicated ribbon, no warping coastline, no fast cuts.

## 4. Copper tree — landscape, 16:9

Reference image: `copper-tree.jpg`

> An eight-second twilight film of a lone tree made from fine glowing copper threads on a still, dark lake. The camera slowly arcs around the tree while a soft pulse of light travels along its branches. A light breeze moves the smallest twigs and faint ripples gently disturb the reflection, then the water settles. Preserve the tree's silhouette and the distant mountain horizon. Deep indigo, copper and violet fine-art cinematography. One calm continuous shot, no people, no text, no logos, no new branches growing, no flicker, no abrupt cuts.
