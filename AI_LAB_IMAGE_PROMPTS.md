# AI Lab image prompts

These are the prompts used to generate the eight images in `public/ai-lab/`. They are kept here for reuse and are not displayed in the gallery. The images were generated with the built-in image generation tool, then exported as optimized JPEG files for the website.

## 1. Stone arch — `stone-arch.jpg`

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: first artwork for an AI creative portfolio gallery. Create a cinematic, dreamlike landscape photograph of a vast pale stone arch rising from a still desert lake at dawn, its inner opening revealing a distant glowing horizon. Elegant editorial fine-art aesthetic, tactile weathered stone, dusty mauve sky, muted sage and warm cream palette, restrained surrealism, atmospheric depth, subtle film grain. Wide landscape composition with strong architectural geometry and no people. This is a standalone artwork, not a webpage or mockup. No text, no logos, no borders, no watermark.
